## Getting credits

1. Finish the exercises. The Open University enrollment link appears in the submission system.
2. Save your UH student number in the submission system.
3. Pass the exam: parts 1–5, 120 minutes, retry allowed after one week. It doesn't affect the grade.
4. Press the blue button twice to request credit registration. Grades appear in Sisu about 4 weeks later.
